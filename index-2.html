SUTD · 30.102 Electromagnetics & Applications · Jun 2026 – Aug 2026

Flexible Liquid-Metal Antenna for Knee Rehabilitation Monitoring

A flexible liquid-metal sinusoidal antenna for wireless, passive knee-angle monitoring in rehabilitation applications, communicating via resonant frequency shifting near 2.4 GHz.

  • Contribution: Designed the sinusoidal dipole antenna and ran CST simulations of it alongside the combined transmitter–receiver system, sweeping bending angle, orientation, and separation distance to optimise performance and study the resulting electromagnetic behaviour.
  • Result: Measured S12 amplitude increased monotonically from −45.15 dB at 0° to −22.26 dB at 100°, giving a reliable one-to-one mapping between knee bend angle and signal strength, with radiation efficiency maintained above 90.6% across the full bending range.
